Subject: selftests/mm: check fixed mremap clears target gaps
Date: Sat, 14 Nov 2026 17:18:09 -0800

The multi-VMA move test unmaps its destination before calling mremap(). 
It therefore misses the case where the source contains a hole and the
corresponding destination page is already mapped.  The fixed move
currently leaves that destination mapping in place after returning success
[1].

Run the existing source-gap case with the destination initially mapped as
well as unmapped, both with and without MREMAP_DONTUNMAP.  After a
successful move, check with mincore() that the destination page
corresponding to the source hole is unmapped.  Also mark a data-pattern
mismatch as a test failure rather than reporting a pass after cleanup.

The two new cases should fail before the mremap fix and pass when a
successful fixed move clears the entire destination range.
