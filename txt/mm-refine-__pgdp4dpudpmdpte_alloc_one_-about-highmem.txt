__{pgd,p4d,pud,pmd,pte}_alloc_one_*() always allocate pages with GFP
flag GFP_PGTABLE_KERNEL/GFP_PGTABLE_USER.

Neither of these include __GFP_HIGHMEM so the clearning of __GFP_HIGHMEM
is unnecessary.
