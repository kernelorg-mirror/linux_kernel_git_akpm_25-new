Subject: ipc/msg: keep expelled receivers from taking new messages
Date: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 12:28:21 +0900

Patch series "ipc/msg: prevent reads after queue permission revocation".

Two races can let a blocked msgrcv() obtain a message sent after IPC_SET
revoked its read permission.  Patch 1 prevents a sender from handing a
message to a receiver that IPC_SET has asked to retry.  Patch 2 makes the
receive permission check atomic with insertion into the receiver list. 
Messages handed off before IPC_SET takes the queue lock retain their
existing behavior.


This patch (of 2):

IPC_SET wakes blocked receivers to recheck read permission, but
expunge_all() leaves them in q_receivers.  Before a receiver runs,
pipelined_send() can replace its -EAGAIN result with a new message.  The
receiver can then obtain data sent after its read access was revoked.

Mark receivers touched by expunge_all() and skip them in pipelined_send().
Leave them on the list until they wake: IPC_RMID must still find them and
change -EAGAIN to -EIDRM before the queue can be freed.  A skipped message
remains on the queue for an authorized receiver.  The resumed receiver
removes itself from the list and retries its permission check.
