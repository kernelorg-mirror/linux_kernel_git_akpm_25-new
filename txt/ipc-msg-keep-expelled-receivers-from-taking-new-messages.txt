Subject: ipc/msg: keep expelled receivers from taking new messages
Date: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 12:28:21 +0900

Patch series "ipc/msg: prevent reads after queue permission revocation".

A process whose read access to a SysV message queue was revoked by
IPC_SET can receive a message sent afterward. A blocked receiver needs
no capability and can have a different UID from the queue owner. Patch 1
prevents delivery to a receiver awakened by IPC_SET. Patch 2 closes the
window between the read permission check and waiter insertion.

IPC_SET continues to wake every blocked receiver, whether its access
changed or not. A receiver with unchanged access leaves and rejoins the
wait list after waking, potentially changing its position as before.
A sender can queue a message instead of handing it to that receiver.


This patch (of 2):

IPC_SET sets a blocked receiver's result to -EAGAIN so it retries its
read permission check. expunge_all() leaves the receiver on q_receivers;
a sender can hand it a new message before it wakes. If IPC_SET revoked
its read access, it receives data that was sent after the revocation.

Mark every receiver that expunge_all() touched and skip it in
pipelined_send().  Keep it linked until it wakes so IPC_RMID can still
find it and change its result to -EIDRM.  The message remains queued. 
After waking, a receiver whose access was revoked gets -EACCES.  The
wake-all behavior and possible wait-list reordering on IPC_SET remain.
