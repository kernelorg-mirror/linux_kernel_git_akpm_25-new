Subject: mm/migrate: flush batched unmaps before unlocking a failed source folio
Date: Tue, 17 Nov 2026 17:49:27 -0800

An asynchronous migration can replace some of a folio's PTEs with
migration entries, defer the TLB flush and then fail to unmap another
mapping.  The failure path restores the remaining migration entries
and unlocks the source folio, but the flush waits until the end of all
retry passes.

A concurrent zap can clear a migration entry.  If truncate then
removes the remaining mappings, a later retry can drop the migration
reference and free the folio before the deferred flush.  A CPU can
retain a writable translation to the freed page and change its next
owner's contents.  Flush the batched unmaps before unlocking a source
folio after a failed unmap.  Also flush before a retry drops its last
reference, in case an earlier path left batched unmaps pending.
