Subject: zsmalloc: switch handle type to struct zs_handle
Date: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 15:46:27 +0900

zsmalloc handle is an unsigned long that holds an address of a
handle_cachep object, so zsmalloc and its users have to cast it back and
forth.  Introduce struct zs_handle and switch zsmalloc, zram and zswap to
struct zs_handle pointers.  Functions that only read the handle take a
const pointer.

This is a preparation for embedding struct zs_handle into zram and
zswap entries.  No functional change.
