Subject: selftests/mm: hmm: speed up migrate_remap_fault
Date: Tue, 24 Nov 2026 00:22:00 -0800

migrate_remap_fault runs all 24 combinations of migration order,
MREMAP_DONTUNMAP, huge-page advice and destination offset on a PMD-sized
range.  With 64K base pages the PMD size is 512MB, and the test takes
about 32 seconds, exceeding its 30-second timeout [1].

The MADV_NOHUGEPAGE cases do not need a PMD-sized range.  Limit those
cases to 2MB while leaving every MADV_HUGEPAGE case PMD-sized.  Keep all
24 combinations and derive each destination offset from its range size.

The two data checks currently run the selftest assertion machinery for
every integer in the range.  Compare each integer directly and invoke
ASSERT_EQ() only when one differs.  Every integer is still checked, and
the assertion still reports the first mismatch.
