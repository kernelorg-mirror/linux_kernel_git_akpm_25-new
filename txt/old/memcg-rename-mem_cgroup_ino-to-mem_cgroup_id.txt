Rename mem_cgroup_ino() to mem_cgroup_id() and mem_cgroup_get_from_ino()
to mem_cgroup_get_from_id().  These functions now use cgroup IDs (from
cgroup_id()) rather than inode numbers, so the names should reflect that.
