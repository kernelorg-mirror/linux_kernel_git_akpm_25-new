Subject: selftests/proc: reduce fd-003-kthread lookup checks
Date: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 23:52:05 -0800

fd-003-kthread performs 6,145 statx() calls against a kernel thread's fd
directory.  Most probe consecutive numbers with the same expected result,
and test_readdir() already checks that the directory is empty.  The long
lookup loops make this selftest slow; one run took about 32 seconds.

Retain short runs around INT_MIN, zero, INT_MAX and UINT_MAX, and check
1023 and 1024 explicitly.  This still exercises ordinary fd numbers,
negative names and the integer boundaries while reducing the number of
statx() calls from 6,145 to 83.

This follows review of Qingshuang Fu's proposed unprivileged-skip change.
