Subject: x86/mm/64: convert p*d_populate{,_init} to _kernel variants
Date: Fri, 25 Jul 2025 10:21:05 +0900

Introduce p*d_populate_kernel_safe() and convert p*d_populate{,_init}() to
p*d_populate_kernel{,_init}() to ensure synchronization of kernel mappings
when populating PGD entries.

By converting them, we eliminate the risk of forgetting to synchronize
top-level page tables after populating PGD entries.
