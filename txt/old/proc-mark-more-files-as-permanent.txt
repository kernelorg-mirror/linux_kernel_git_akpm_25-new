Mark
	/proc/devices
	/proc/kpagecount
	/proc/kpageflags
	/proc/kpagecgroup
	/proc/loadavg
	/proc/meminfo
	/proc/softirqs
	/proc/uptime
	/proc/version

as permanent /proc entries, saving alloc/free and some list/spinlock ops
per use.

These files are never removed by the kernel so it is OK to mark them.
