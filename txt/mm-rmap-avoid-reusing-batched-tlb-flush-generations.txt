Subject: mm/rmap: avoid reusing batched TLB flush generations
Date: Tue, 17 Nov 2026 17:49:27 -0800

mm->tlb_flush_batched resets to 1 when its 15-bit pending count grows
large.  A concurrent flush can read 1, flush, then see 1 again after the
reset and mark newer unmaps as flushed.  A subsequent caller can skip a
needed TLB flush and leave a stale writable translation.

Use the existing atomic_t as two bits instead of counting generations. 
PENDING records a deferred unmap.  A caller atomically changes PENDING to
FLUSHING before flushing.  Any other caller seeing FLUSHING flushes for
itself rather than treating the first flush as complete.  An unmap that
arrives during the flush sets PENDING again.  The first caller clears only
FLUSHING after its flush, preserving that new work.  There are no
generation values to wrap or for a delayed flusher to write back, and
mm_struct does not grow.

Order the PTE clear before setting PENDING.  Initialize the state for a
new mm: dup_mm() copies the parent's mm_struct before mm_init(), so the
child must not inherit an in-progress flush with no owner.
