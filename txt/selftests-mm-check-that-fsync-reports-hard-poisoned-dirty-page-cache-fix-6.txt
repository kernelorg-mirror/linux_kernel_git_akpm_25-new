Subject: selftests/mm: write the whole page-cache test file for OMFS
Date: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 10:46:15 -0800

Fix-5 grows the page-cache test file by writing one byte at the end.
That works on HPFS, but leaves a hole on OMFS, whose extent allocation
does not support holes. After a mapped write and fsync(), the file still
has the expected contents in cache. On remount, 2048 of its 4096 bytes
have changed, so the hard-clean-page test fails when poisoning drops
the cached page and rereads the file.

Write a full page of zeroes from offset zero. This still grows the file
on HPFS and allocates all its blocks on OMFS before the test maps it.
Check the write and clean up on failure as before. In a diagnostic OMFS
guest, the test passed 9 of 10 cases with fix-5 and 10 of 10 with the
full-page write.
