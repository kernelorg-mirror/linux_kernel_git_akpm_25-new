Subject: MAINTAINERS: cover unclaimed MM infrastructure under MISC
Date: Sun, 01 Nov 2026 18:30:41 -0800

Files without a clear MM component are labeled unclaimed and assigned to
MISC.  Add the files listed below without removing them from any existing
MAINTAINERS entry.

Assign allocation code-tag instrumentation to ALLOCATION PROFILING and
stack-depot storage used by KASAN to KASAN.  Include their public headers.
The generic interval-tree implementation and test remain under MISC with
include/linux/interval_tree.h; the reverse-mapping template is assigned to
RMAP in patch 1.  Include the memory-region ID interface alongside its
implementation.  General bitmap, rbtree, objpool, FORTIFY, fault-injection,
KHO, IOMMU and scatterlist files are not assigned to MM by this patch.

Add the following file assignments:

MEMORY MANAGEMENT - ALLOCATION PROFILING (ALLOC TAG)
        include/linux/codetag.h
        lib/codetag.c

MEMORY MANAGEMENT - KASAN
        include/linux/stackdepot.h
        lib/stackdepot.c

MEMORY MANAGEMENT - MISC
        include/linux/genalloc.h
        include/linux/interval_tree.h
        include/linux/io-mapping.h
        include/linux/mem_encrypt.h
        include/linux/memregion.h
        lib/devres.c
        lib/genalloc.c
        lib/interval_tree.c
        lib/interval_tree_test.c
        lib/memregion.c
        mm/Makefile
