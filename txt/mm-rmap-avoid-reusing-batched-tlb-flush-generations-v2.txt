Subject: mm/rmap: avoid reusing batched TLB flush generations v2
Date: Tue, 17 Nov 2026 17:49:27 -0800

mm->tlb_flush_batched resets to 1 when its 15-bit pending count grows
large.  A concurrent flush can read 1, flush, then see 1 again after
the reset and mark newer unmaps as flushed.  A subsequent zap can skip
a needed TLB flush and leave a stale writable translation.

Use separate 64-bit pending and flushed counts.  An unmap during a
flush leaves them unequal.  Concurrent flushers can move the flushed
count backwards, causing an extra flush, but cannot hide an unmap.
