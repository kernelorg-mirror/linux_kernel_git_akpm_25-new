Subject: ocfs2: skip network flush when no domain handlers are registered
Date: Sun, 15 Nov 2026 06:55:10 -0800

Cen's domain-handler cleanup drains the o2net workqueue before destroying
dlm_worker.  The handler list can be empty on registration failure before
the first handler is installed.  A partial registration failure can also
call dlm_unregister_domain_handlers() twice: once in the registration
error path and again in the join error path.  The second call finds an
empty list.

An empty list has no domain handlers left to drain.  Avoid taking
o2net_wq_mutex and flushing unrelated receive work in these cases, as
Joseph Qi requested [1].  Keep the unregister-and-flush operation for a
nonempty list so in-flight handlers finish before dlm_worker is destroyed.
