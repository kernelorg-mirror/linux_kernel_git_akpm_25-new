MAX_ORDER is not inclusive: the maximum allocation order buddy allocator
can deliver is MAX_ORDER-1.

Fix MAX_ORDER usage in floppy code.

Also allocation buffer exactly PAGE_SIZE << MAX_ORDER bytes is okay. Fix
MAX_LEN check.
