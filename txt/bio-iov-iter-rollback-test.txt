Subject: block: restore iov_iter when bio extraction rejects a bio
Date: Sun, 18 Oct 2026 17:30:00 -0700

bio_iov_iter_get_pages() may successfully extract one or more iovec
segments before a later segment fails alignment validation.  In that
case it discards the bio and returns -EINVAL, but leaves the iterator
advanced past the extracted segments.

An iomap direct-I/O caller can subsequently reexpand the iterator's
count and fall back to buffered I/O.  Because the iterator's segment
position was not restored, its count then exceeds the data available
in the remaining segments.  This can cause iterate_iovec() to read
past the end of the imported iovec array.

Restore the iterator state when the entire bio is rejected for an
alignment error.  A full snapshot is needed because advancing past
empty iovecs cannot reliably be undone by reverting a byte count.

This is a test patch, independent of the alignment-validation change
in iov_iter_extract_bvecs().
