From: Andrew Morton <akpm@linux-foundation.org>
Subject: mm/migrate_device: allocate deferred split sublists for new huge folios
Date: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 09:24:38 -0800

migrate_vma_insert_huge_pmd_page() can populate an anonymous mapping
with a newly charged large folio. Unlike the normal anonymous THP
allocation paths, it does not allocate the charged memcg's deferred
split sublist.

If the memcg has no sublist, partially unmapping the folio makes
deferred_split_folio() fall back to an ancestor's list. This warns
because the fallback is intended for dying cgroups. If a subsequent
normal THP allocation creates the missing sublist, freeing the earlier
folio removes it from the ancestor's list while holding the child's
lock and updating the child's count. The accounting is then wrong,
and concurrent deferred split scanning can corrupt the list, leading
to kernel crashes or hangs.

The list_lru conversion added sublist allocation to anonymous faults,
swapin and collapse, but missed this path, which creates new folios
rather than merely transferring an existing charge during migration.

Allocate the deferred split sublist after charging and before installing
the PMD. On failure, use the existing abort path to clear the migration
flags and leave the destination folio for migration finalization to
release. Count this as an allocation fallback rather than a memcg
charge failure.

Based on Ada's supplied fix and reproducer. The report uses CONFIG_TEST_HMM
and privileged guest setup; ordinary-user reachability through a production
driver has not been established.

Built matching pre-patch and patched 7.4.0-rc1 kernels and ran the same
one-folio HMM migration test in each. The x86-64 KVM guest had 8 GiB RAM,
one virtual NUMA node and four single-threaded vCPUs in one virtual socket.
CONFIG_TEST_HMM, CONFIG_MEMCG, CONFIG_SHRINKER_DEBUG and CONFIG_DEBUG_LIST
were enabled; KASAN was disabled.

Both kernels migrated 512 pages to device memory and one huge folio back.
After a partial unmap, normal THP allocation and freeing the migrated
folio, the pre-patch kernel still reported one deferred-split entry on
the root list. The patched kernel reported no entry. Both guests completed
without a panic or warning. Concurrent list scanning was not stressed.
