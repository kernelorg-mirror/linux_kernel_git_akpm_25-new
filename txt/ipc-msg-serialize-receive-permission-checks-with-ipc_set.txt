Subject: ipc/msg: serialize receive permission checks with IPC_SET
Date: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 12:28:21 +0900

do_msgrcv() checks read permission before taking the queue lock.  IPC_SET
can revoke access and pass through expunge_all() between that check and
insertion into q_receivers.  The new receiver can then sleep through the
revocation and accept a later message, even if senders skip receivers that
IPC_SET found on the list.

Check read permission under the same queue lock that protects IPC_SET and
waiter insertion, as do_msgsnd() already does for write permission.  On
denial, release the lock through the existing out_unlock0 path.
