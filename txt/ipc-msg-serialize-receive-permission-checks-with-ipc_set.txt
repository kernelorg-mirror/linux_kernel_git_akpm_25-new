Subject: ipc/msg: serialize receive permission checks with IPC_SET
Date: Sun, 22 Nov 2026 18:37:35 -0800

do_msgrcv() checks read permission before taking the queue lock.  IPC_SET
can revoke access after that check and before the receiver joins
q_receivers.  It then misses the IPC_SET wakeup and can receive a later
message despite losing access.

Take the queue lock before the receive loop so the permission check,
message lookup and insertion into q_receivers are serialized with IPC_SET.
The receiver releases the lock before sleeping.  On an -EAGAIN wakeup it
reacquires the lock, removes itself from q_receivers, and keeps the lock
for the next permission check.  The earlier revision [1] unlocked at the
bottom of the loop only to lock again at the top.
